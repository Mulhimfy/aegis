Aegiss

See what your phone leaves open.

It reads the phone. Not a checklist of advice: the actual state of this device, right now, said in a sentence that names what is true.

Then it takes you to the exact system settings screen that fixes it, and re-checks itself when you come back.

What it tells you

Missing 7 months of security patches.

2 apps can read everything on your screen.

The screen never locks itself.

This network has no password, so nothing is protected.

Most people have never seen any of that written down about their own phone.

The rules it holds to

  • Nothing leaves the device. No server, no account, no analytics. An app that reads your security posture and then uploaded it would be the thing it warns you about.
  • Nothing is guessed. When the operating system refuses to disclose a signal, the check reports as unreadable and drops out of the score rather than being assumed.
  • It cannot change anything. Every fix is you, in your own settings. The app holds no permission to alter the device.
  • Questions the OS will not answer are asked out loud. Answers expire after 90 days, because a phone configured correctly last year is not evidence about the phone today.
